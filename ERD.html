Notes

  • XP is a ledger. xp_events is the source of truth; the trigger trg_sync_user_xp recomputes users.total_xp on every insert. kind ∈ {lesson, correct, review}.
  • Logical (unenforced) references live in code: xp_events.exercise_id and mistakes.exercise_id point into the modules.exercises[] JSON; user_achievements.key maps to the achievement catalogue in backend/lib/gamification.js.
  • Legacy / compat columns: modules.category (alias of the domain slug) and modules.challenge (v1 single-challenge JSON) are kept for backward compatibility.
  • Not in the database: hearts (client-side localStorage) and auth tokens (stateless 30-day JWTs).
